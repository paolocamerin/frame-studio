Drawing the drops

  1. Keep each drop inside its box (40 × 48 px from the top edge, centred on the button).
  2. Leave room for the 12 × 12 icon, or draw it too.
  3. One layer per drop; black and white only, transparent where the artwork shows.
  4. Export each layer as a full-canvas PNG.
